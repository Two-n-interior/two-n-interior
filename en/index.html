ABOUT US

two n interior was founded in 2024 (B.E. 2567) by a management team and staff with more than 15 years of continuous experience in design, interior decoration, construction, renovation, and project development.

We focus on providing interior design and space renovation services, applying real hands-on experience from managing actual projects — residential homes, offices, retail stores, and large-scale developments — into every stage of our work: from concept development, site survey and analysis, design, material selection, and built-in furniture design, through to construction, site supervision, and project handover.

Our team has experience managing projects ranging in value from several million baht up to 100 million baht, giving us a deep understanding of the design process, renovation planning, budget management, coordination with contractors and stakeholders, and quality control at every stage.

"We believe that great interior design and renovation work isn't only about beauty — it must meet real functional needs, reflect the identity of the people who use the space, and fit within the budget."

Every project therefore begins with a thorough understanding of the client's needs, together with a survey of the existing site conditions and constraints, before developing a design concept and renovation plan suited to the space and the client's lifestyle. We place importance on function, space planning, materials, lighting, colour, building systems, and fine detailing, so that every space is improved efficiently, beautifully, practically, and with long-term quality.

Although the company was founded in 2024, the work of two n interior is driven by a professional team with expertise in interior design and decoration, renovation and space improvement, built-in furniture, construction, and building systems. We are ready to take care of every project — from consultation, site survey, design, and planning, through production, installation, construction, site supervision, and a systematic handover of the finished work.
